## The world

- **No islands, no water.** One big map like Breath of the Wild's: different areas with different themes (meadow, forest,
  ice, fire, water, …), **ordered by tier** outward from the start.
- **Normal spots** are most of the map: you fight level-appropriate mobs of that area. **Mobs live in their own parts of
  the map**, so you always know what you are going after. A normal spot also has a chance of something else: a chest to
  open, a wandering merchant, a mini-boss, a gambling event.
- **Special spots**: a treasure chest, a potion spot, a spells spot.
- **Shops** sell the basic versions of the weapons (and armour) of that area's tier.
- **Random mini-bosses and other events spawn on random normal spots from time to time.**
- **Touching a spot gives hints** on what you could meet (level range, loot rewards), but not everything: plenty of
  surprise.
- **No town liberation** (Dokapon's). This is an MMO-lite: go around, kill mobs, get better, kill bosses, get further.

## Bosses

- **Bosses are seen on the board.** Nothing gates them but your power: too low a level and too poor gear, and the fight is
  simply too hard.
- **Bosses are the gates to new areas**: they stand on the spot that leads into the next area. **Land exactly on the boss**
  to fight it. Once it is dead, that part of the map opens.
- **Bosses can be killed again**, with better loot. A boss is back **5 minutes** after it died.

## Gear and inventory

- **Gear is randomized and tiered** like WoW's or Diablo's: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary. **Every piece has a
  rank by level** (copper sword, iron sword, …), and you can find **a named version** ("Stormborn", an iron sword with a
  special effect) that feels special.
- **Equipment slots: weapon, armour, helm, boots, trinket.**
- **A simple inventory**: keep the weapons and armour you found and switch to whatever you want; a bag for potions and
  the like.

## Fighting

- **A turn-based RPG, not Dokapon.** Attacking normally, using items, casting spells and abilities, charging; and
  **a defensive choice before the enemy attacks** (different kinds).
- **Anyone can charge** for a bigger attack next turn.
- **Classes have basic abilities that fit their style**, different enough that the play differs; **more skills and higher
  ranks are bought, found or earned** as you go.
- **Mana for everyone** is the resource for abilities.
- **Death**: back to the nearest town, a fifth of the gold lost, gear kept (unchanged from the brief).

## Names

- **The working title is Zite.** World and place names are the Game Developer's to choose for now and can change later.
- How many areas are laid by hand before the generator is the Game Developer's call (three to start: mine).

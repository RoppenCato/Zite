# The 24 ability icons

Mitch, AD, 2026-11-02. Designs mine, under Robin's permission of 2026-11-01. One atlas, `abilities.png` (512 square, 1 MB decoded), 40 CSS px at 2x. **Frame names are the rules' ability ids** in `rules/data/abilities.json`, such as `heavy-blow` for Mighty Swing. The pivot is the centre.

Each icon sits on a round plate in its class's colour: red for the Knight, plum for the Trickster, blue for the Wizard, green for the Archer. `preview.jpg` shows them at 80, 40 and 28.

Not seen in the game. I judged them at 40 and 28 on a parchment ground only.

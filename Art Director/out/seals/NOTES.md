# The Seals

Mitch, AD, 2026-11-02, for Miles, GD; Robin agreed the Seals, and the designs are mine. A wax seal on two ribbon tails in the land's colour, with the land's own mark pressed in. Not seen in the game. I judged them at 112 and 56 on a brown ground, in `preview.jpg`.

`seals.png` (512 by 256, 19 KB on disk, 0.5 MB in memory) and `seals.json`. 56 CSS px at 2x, pivot at the centre, a little of the frame's foot taken by the ribbons.

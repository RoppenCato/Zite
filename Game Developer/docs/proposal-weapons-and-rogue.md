# Weapons by class, and a new Rogue

Miles, GD, 2026-11-03, for Robin, from his phone test. **A proposal: nothing here is built yet.** Your choices go first; then the build, Mitch, AD's weapon art, and a retune of all four classes.

## Why

Today every class can wear every weapon. The game has three gear lines: heavy is your class's own weapon, light is a Club and a Buckler, and magic is a Staff and a Tome. A Rogue's damage therefore has no ceiling we can plan around: three hits an attack, poison that grows with every hit, and crit on top. Your playtest showed it: at level 5 a poison Rogue was far too strong.

## 1. Weapon types, and who wields what

The shield slot becomes the **off-hand**, and each class has its own off-hand piece.

| Weapon | Hands | Feel | Warrior | Rogue | Magician | Hunter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sword | one | even, safe | **yes** | **yes** | | |
| Great weapon (axe, greatsword, maul) | two | slow, huge hits, no off-hand | **yes** | | | |
| Dagger | one | quick, crits, poison | | **yes** | | **yes** |
| Bow | two | strikes from afar, shoots back | | | | **yes** |
| Staff | two | big spells, more mana | | | **yes** | |
| Wand | one | cheap, quick spells, more crit | | | **yes** | |

| Off-hand | Who | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shield | Warrior with a sword | defense; Block stops more |
| Parrying dagger | Rogue | dodge; a hit after a dodge |
| Orb or tome | Magician with a wand | mana and spell power |
| Quiver | Hunter with a bow (the one two-hander with an off-hand) | more shots back |

That gives every class two weapons and a real choice:

- **Warrior: sword and shield** (Knight, safe) **or a great weapon** (Berserker, big hits, less defense). That is your idea. Paladin works with either.
- **Rogue: dagger** (Shadow, Alchemist) **or sword** (Ninja).
- **Magician: staff** (Wizard: big spells) **or wand and orb** (quick spells, Spellsword).
- **Hunter: bow** (Ranger, Trapper) **or dagger** (close in; the owl and the traps work with both). You also suggested throwing weapons for the Hunter. I'd leave them out: the bow already shoots, and a dagger gives a real close-range choice.

The legendary weapons already fit: Dawnbreaker is a great weapon, Emberbrand and Winter's Edge are swords, and Viperfang, Nightsong and Redtide are daggers.

## 2. Skills that need a weapon

A skill that is plainly about a weapon only works with it. Its button stays dim and says why.

- **Warrior:** Shield Bonk, Castle Stance and the Knight's shield skills need a shield. The Berserker's swings hit harder with a great weapon.
- **Rogue:** poison needs a dagger, and blood needs a blade (sword or dagger).
- **Magician:** Falling Star and Firestorm need a staff, and the Spellsword's blade spells need a wand.
- **Hunter:** Triple Shot, Bullseye and the Ranger's arrows need a bow.

## 3. Weapons that aren't yours

You asked whether a Rogue should still be able to swing a great axe for fun. I think yes:

- **Drops lean to your class:** about 3 in 4 weapons that drop are yours, and the rest you can sell or try.
- **Anyone can wear anything,** but an unfamiliar weapon hits **20% softer** and gets none of your class's strengths. A Rogue with an axe swings once, not three times, and gets no poison.

So it stays fun to try but never beats your own. *(My choice: the 20% and the 3 in 4.)*

## 4. A new Rogue (from your notes)

**Poison stops growing.** Today each poisoned hit adds to the sting, so it climbs every round. Instead, poison is a steady sting that a new hit renews, not stacks. Being poisoned becomes a state other skills feed on.

**The core moves, one for each tree.** Grand Finale stays in every Rogue's kit, and each tree has a passive that changes it:

- **Alchemist:** on a poisoned foe it bursts the poison, with extra damage for each point of Mischief.
- **Shadow:** each point of Mischief adds a crit chance to the finale, and the finale hits 3 times.
- **Ninja:** it spends your blood (below), each drop adding a bleed.

**The trees:**

| Tree | Weapon | What it is about | Out | In (examples) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shadow** | dagger | many small hits, crits | | **Thousand Cuts** (6 small hits, each can crit); crits build Mischief; Night Cut, Assassinate, Reaper stay |
| **Alchemist** | dagger | poison set up, then cashed in | the growing poison | Poison Coating (a steady sting); **Toxic Jab** (more on a poisoned foe); Bitter End (cash in all the poison now); flasks stay |
| **Ninja** | sword or dagger | **blood** | Shuriken, Kunai Storm, Falling Lotus | **Blood**: each hit leaves a drop on your blade, up to 3. **Crimson Arc** (a cut that spends blood: harder per drop), **Bleed Out** (opens a bleed), **Red Mist** (blood heals you), Shadow Clone stays |

**Blood and poison together (high level):** a late talent, **Black Blood**, makes a foe that is both bleeding and poisoned take each sting twice. That's the combo for a player who puts points in both trees.

**Balance limits (mine):** crit chance tops out at 60%, and a poisoned hit never makes the sting bigger. I'd rerun all four classes to level 30 after the change.

## 5. A day and night, counted in rolls

You asked whether there should be one. I think yes, kept light:

- **A day is 20 rolls and a night is 10,** so one full turn takes about 15 to 20 minutes of play.
- **At night the map darkens,** and its creatures are 15% stronger but give 20% more XP and gold. A few things happen only at night: some rumours, and a night merchant.
- **Resting in a town can sleep you to morning,** if you want to.
- **The top bar shows a sun or a moon:** "Day 4 · dusk".
- **Weekly events stay on real days,** like the Arena on Monday, Thursday and Saturday.
- **This replaces the brief's idea** of real-world time of day changing the monsters. Real weather could still come later.

# The ability icons

Mitch, AD, 2026-11-02: 30 new icons for Robin's classes and paths, and `snare` redrawn as Pinning Shot, an arrow, not a net. Designs mine, under Robin's permission of 2026-11-01. One atlas, `abilities.png` (512 square, 1 MB decoded), 40 CSS px at 2x. **Frame names are the rules' ability ids** in `rules/data/abilities.json`, such as `heavy-blow` for Mighty Swing. The pivot is the centre.

Each icon sits on a round plate in its class's colour: red for the Warrior, plum for the Rogue, blue for the Magician, green for the Hunter. `preview.jpg` shows them at 80, 40 and 28.

Not seen in the game. I judged them at 40 and 28 on a parchment ground only.

## New on 2026-11-02, for the trees

- **The always-on rules** sit in a gold-edged crest, so they read as something you are, not something you press.
- **The signatures at the foot of each tree** have a gold ring, including `bulwark`, `meteor`, `arc-slash`, `return-fire` and `spike-trap`.
- **Out of the atlas, nothing uses them:** `war-cry`, `rally`, `poison-blade`, `smoke-bomb`, `frost-bolt`, `mana-shield`, `ember`, `renew`, `night-veil`, `owl-watch`. They stay in the tool, so they can come back in a moment.

## New on 2026-11-03

- **`magic-power`**, Magic Power: a charged violet gem with rays and two gold chevrons going up. It is for Robin's Magic Power, in place of the Pocket Watch on the button. `time-warp` stays in the atlas until Miles, GD says it can go.

# The brief for a master illustration (approach 3)

Mitch, AD, 2026-11-01. If Robin picks approach 3, the Warrior and the Slime are painted once each, outside the code, from
this brief: by Robin's image tool, or by an illustrator. I cut the master into parts, give each a pivot, and animate the
parts from pose data, so every frame is the master's own pixels and nothing flickers. What matters is below; the words
in the prompt block are a starting point, not a formula.

## What I need from the picture

1. **One character, one picture, side view facing right, standing.** The arms a little away from the body and the sword
   held out, so every part is visible and cuttable: the head, the hair, the torso, the near arm with the sword, the far
   arm, each leg with its boot. No part hidden behind another.
2. **A plain flat background** in one colour nothing in the character uses (pure magenta `#ff00ff` or a transparent PNG).
   Hard edges, no soft glow or blur round the figure: soft keyed edges were what doubled our atlases before.
3. **At least 1500 pixels tall**, so the parts survive cutting and scaling down to 300 pixels in the game.
4. **The look:** simple and clean. A dark, even outline that tapers a little; flat saturated colours with one shade tone per
   material and a small highlight on shiny things only; no painted texture, no gradients, no realism. Chibi, about two and a
   half heads tall, a big round head, big eyes with a coloured iris and a highlight, spiky hair. Friendly and confident.
5. **The Warrior:** a young man; spiky orange hair; a blue tunic with a brown belt and a gold buckle; brown trousers; big
   dark-brown boots; a plain straight sword with a gold guard, held in the near hand, blade up and forward.
6. **The Slime:** one round drop-shaped body, green, a small drip on top, a huge face on its front: two big eyes with dark
   pupils and a highlight, an open smiling mouth with a pink tongue, pink cheeks. Facing left.
7. **Then two more sheets for each**, in the same picture style and size: (a) the face sheet: the same head with eyes
   closed, a hurt face, and a dead face (crossed eyes or spirals); (b) for the Warrior, the near arm and the sword alone
   in three angles (down, forward, up), so the swing has real drawings to pass through.

## A prompt to start from

> A single 2D game character sprite, side view facing right, standing still, arms slightly away from the body, holding a
> sword out. Clean cartoon illustration, simple shapes, a dark even outline, flat saturated colours with one shadow tone per
> material, no texture, no gradients, no background, flat magenta background #ff00ff, hard edges. Chibi proportions,
> about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, big eyes with green irises and a highlight, spiky orange hair, blue tunic, brown
> belt with a gold buckle, brown trousers, big dark brown boots, a straight sword with a gold guard held blade up. Friendly,
> confident expression. Full body visible, nothing cropped, centred, 1500 px tall.

For the Slime, the same instructions with: *a round green slime monster, a drop shape with a small drip on top, a huge
friendly face on its front, big eyes with dark pupils and a white highlight, open smiling mouth with a pink tongue, pink
cheeks, facing left.*

## What to send me

The pictures as they come out, unedited, into `Examples/masters/` or the chat. I do the cutting, the pivots, the clean-up
of the edges and the atlas. Judge them first the way the game will show them: shrink one to 150 pixels tall on your phone
and see whether it still reads.

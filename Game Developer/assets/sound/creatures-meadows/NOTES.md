# The meadows' creature voices (2026-11-01)

Attack, hit and die for the six creatures of Cloverfield Meadows, plus King Goo's surge: 19 sounds, one variation each (Miles, GD). **All from recordings already on this PC**, in the Survival studio: rubberduck's 80 CC0 creature SFX (1 and 2), HaelDB's male grunts (CC0), a squish pack from OpenGameArt (CC0) and that studio's cuts. Nothing was downloaded. King Goo's crown has one small synth bell.

**Listen:** `preview.mp3` (18 s); on the phone page, Chapter 1, the row "The meadows' creatures".

| Creature (game's id) | Time | attack | hit | die |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jelly Slime (`slime`) | 0:00 | a squelch | a squish | a squish and a splat |
| Grumpy Sprout (`wolf`) | 0:02 | a small grumble | a twig's snap and a squeak | a voice wilting |

**The names** are `creature/<id>/attack`, `hit`, `die`, and `creature/thornwood-warden/surge`.

**Levels** against the die's land: attack -4 dB, hit -5, die and King Goo -2, so a voice sits under the blow it comes with. Peak -5.2 dBFS. Almost all of it is above 250 Hz, so a phone keeps it: the most low end is the surge at 35 %.

**The file:** `game/creatures-meadows.ogg`, 9.9 s, 83 KB, with `creatures-meadows.json`.

How to test a sound in the game
  1. Hear it here first: the tab named in my message (often Chapter 1), the play button on its row; single sounds under Sounds, one button per variation.
  2. Pick it in the game: on the PC, with the game’s workbench running, open localhost:8765/dist/combat-arena.html (the Combat Arena).
  3. In the panel on the right open The clock, the eye and the sounds, then The sounds. Each “Choose …” row has a drop-down of letters: pick a letter and it plays and becomes your choice; Hear it plays it again. The empty choice keeps the old sound.
  4. When you are happy, press Copy beside Your picks, as text and paste it to Clive, GD (or to me): that makes them the game’s.
  5. The weather, in place: in the game press Esc, open Admin, press Weather: … (next) until it says shower, rain, storm or snow (it fades in over 12 to 90 s; a storm’s thunder starts after about a minute). Stand on the wreck’s beach to hear rain on the water. Season: … (turn it) to winter, then walk, for the snow steps; after rain, the wet sand at the waterline prints and puddles lie on paths for the splash.

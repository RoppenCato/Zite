# The world as a world: the plan

Robin, 2026-11-02, after playing chapter 1: "The world needs to feel more like a world, right now its just like a pattern. It
needs to feel more open world... different areas of the map, so its visually interesing and obvious that there are different
places. Dokapon does this by having a beginner map first and then moving to the big world. It should feel more like a big
connected world, where some bridges etc cant be passed unless killing the boss... If for example an area is The castle, then
there should be some spots around that area that belongs to the castle, and the board should make it visually obvious."
He also asked for symbols on every spot, blended graphics with mountains or water between areas, more detail, and for the Art
Director to research world maps in similar games.

## What it means (mine, for Robin to judge)

1. **A beginner map, then the big world.** Cloverfield Meadows stays the start, its own land. King Goo holds the bridge off it.
2. **One connected land past that bridge**, with coast and sea round it, no rectangles. Regions are told apart by their ground and
   shaped by rivers, mountains and shores. A boss holds each bridge or pass; beaten, it opens. Closed land stays hidden by
   nothing square: you can see where you are not yet allowed.
3. **Places, not spots.** A named place (a town, a castle, a grove) has its own ground, buildings and a road of spots that clearly
   belong to it.
4. **Roads that wander**, not loops and straight rungs.
5. **A symbol on every spot** telling you what it does.

The rules do not change: the seeded graph, hubs and links, bosses as gates, areas by tier. Hub ids stay, so saves carry over and the
board is laid again. Grown areas beyond Honeyhill come after the three hand-laid ones are right.

## The order

1. Mitch, AD researches and shows Robin one concept still. Robin says yes or redirects.
2. Mitch, AD delivers the layout (hub positions and road bows) with the terrain kit and spot symbols.
3. I build the board from it: terrain with blended edges, sea, bridges barred until opened, places, the camera over the new shape.
4. Grown areas follow the same way, once Robin likes the first three.

## Robin's word on places (2026-11-02)

"yes that's it, creatures only in their own place. That also means the world probably need to be a lot bigger to actually feel like a world and that there are actual areas. You shouldnt generally wanna just spin around in the same 10 spots over and over for hours."

So: **places like MMO zones.** A creature lives only in its own place (in the rules since this date). You can see where a place starts and ends. Each has its own level range and drops, and quests send you there. **A much bigger world**: today's three maps hold 187 spots; a player should move on to new ground as they level, not circle the same loop. Walking that far by die will need a way to travel back (my thought: a road between towns or a ferry, opened as you go). It will also want more creatures than a region has now, or variants of them; Mitch, AD is to say what that costs.

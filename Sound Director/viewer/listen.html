How to test a sound
  1. Hear it here first: the tab named in my message (Chapter 1 to begin with), the play button on its row; single sounds under Sounds, one button per variation. A phone speaker is the right judge: that is where the game plays.
  2. Pick by letter: tell me, or Miles, GD, the letter you like (A, B or C), or say what is wrong with all of them. The one you pick becomes the game’s; the others are cleared away.
  3. In the game: once Miles, GD has the audio layer and a sound panel in the game, this list says where to find the row.

# The Honeyhill Fields board kit

Mitch, AD, 2026-11-02. Designs mine, under Robin's permission of 2026-11-01. Copy the whole folder. Same form as the forest's: sizes are world pixels, files at 2x, drawn at 0.5. `sheet.jpg` shows every piece.

## Your kit entry (theme `harvest`, area id `frostpeak`)

- **Atlases:** `board_props` (1024 by 2048, 8 MB decoded) and `board_ground` (1024 by 512, 2 MB, the plain tiles, if anything still asks for them).
- **Shared with the meadows:** `cover.png`, `name_plate.png` and `area_banner.png`, as the forest does.
- **Road, outer first:** 30 px `#b4935a`, 25 px `#d9bb7e`, 10 px `#ecd49c` at 0.8 alpha.
- **Flat props, drawn before the roads:** `clover`, `worn`.
- **Placement:** `out/world_harvest/placement.json` under `frostpeak`.
- **Plates:** the same `spot_*` names as the other regions, all eleven kinds, in `board_props`. This replaces `out/board_harvest_pieces`.
